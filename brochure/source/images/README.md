## Rules that must survive into the brochure

1. **Label 01–07 "AI-generated concept visualisation".** `08_master_plan.png` is not AI and needs no
   such label; credit it as drawn from the survey.
2. **The stream is a stream, never the Narmada and never "riverside".** Measured width near the
   colony: median **9.8 m**, 5.9–17.5 m typical, up to 50.5 m at bend pools. The older brief's
   "about 10 ft (3 m)" is wrong.
3. **`04_streamside_garden.png` shows a proposed check dam and permanent pool.** That is not built.
   Caption it as a proposal, not as the current state.
4. **`07_resort_cottages.png` is the resort land.** The 61 colony plots must stay detached (Rule 53,
   3 m side setbacks); a row of cottages across plot lines would be group housing.
5. **`03b` places the boulevard directly beside the stream, which the plan does not** — the three
   garden medians are on the *interior* boulevards. Use it as a boulevard/median view, never caption
   it as the stream frontage. `04_streamside_garden.png` is the correct stream-edge image.
6. **Houses are ~1,000 sq ft out-houses**, not permanent homes, and must never be drawn filling a plot.
7. Every Flow image carries Google's small provenance watermark bottom-right. Do not remove it.
8. Imagery credit where satellite is visible: **Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics**. Stream centreline:
   © OpenStreetMap contributors.
