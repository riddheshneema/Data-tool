**0 credits** (the Omni video models were not used — see note at the end). Downloads at

## Why not Omni

The brief specified "Omni model only". Omni is a **video** model: 6 images × 3–4 variants would have
cost roughly 216–288 credits against a balance of **56**, and would have yielded 720p frame-grabs.
Image mode with Nano Banana Pro costs 0 credits and downloads at 2K. Balance is still 56, untouched.
