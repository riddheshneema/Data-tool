## 1. Thresholds and coverage: plotted developments, farmhouse/agricultural plots, rural (gram panchayat) areas, and MP RERA decisions on farmhouse projects

### Takeaway
A plotted colony is squarely a "real estate project" under RERA. A farmhouse-plot scheme with a common layout and shared roads, power and water is treated the same way. Registration is mandatory unless the project falls under the section 3(2)(a) exemption (land of 500 sq m or less, or 8 or fewer units, counting all phases). The live legal questions for Jalkoti are not about plots versus farmhouses. They are (a) whether the site lies within a "planning area", the wording section 3(1) uses, and (b) the "or" reading of the exemption. I found no published MP RERA order specifically on farmhouse or "farm land" schemes.

### Cited Findings
- **Definition:** The Act defines a "real estate project" to include "the development of land into plots for the purpose of selling all or some of the said plots". A developer creating plots for sale is covered even if nothing is built on them. — [99acres (citing s.2)](https://www.99acres.com/articles/rera-applicability-plots-farmhouses.html); Act text: [RERA Act 2016, India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158)
- **Section 3(1) prohibition (Act text):** No promoter may "advertise, market, book, sell or offer for sale, or invite persons to purchase in any manner any plot, apartment or building ... in any real estate project or part of it, in any planning area" without registering with the Authority. — [RERA Act 2016, India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158) (wording from the statute; not re-fetched this session)
- **Section 3(2)(a) exemption:** No registration is needed where the land to be developed does not exceed 500 sq m, or the number of apartments does not exceed eight, inclusive of all phases. A proviso lets the appropriate Government reduce the threshold below 500 sq m or 8 apartments. — [iPleaders summary of RERA](https://blog.ipleaders.in/real-estate-regulation-and-development-act-2016-2/); [Mondaq / Argus Partners, "RERA regime: the exemption conundrum"](https://argus-p.com/papers-publications/thought-paper/rera-regime-the-exemption-conundrum/)
- **Sources disagree on how the two limits combine.** One commentary says a project must meet both limits to be exempt. The Argus Partners paper reports that Odisha exempts a project with more than 500 sq m of land but no more than 8 apartments, and that Goa's SOP makes a project registrable only when it exceeds both limits. — [Argus Partners](https://argus-p.com/papers-publications/thought-paper/rera-regime-the-exemption-conundrum/); [legalsuvidha](https://legalsuvidha.com/blog/rera-applicability-of-rera-act)
- **Phases:** Under the explanation to section 3, each phase of a phased project is a standalone project for registration purposes, and the exemption counts units across all phases. — Act text, [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158)
- **Farmhouses (secondary source):** A farmhouse sold as an individual unit, or as raw agricultural land without internal roads, drainage or water supply, is outside RERA. Multiple farmhouses under a unified plan with shared infrastructure (roads, electricity, sewage, parks) need registration once they cross the threshold. — [99acres, May 2025](https://www.99acres.com/articles/rera-applicability-plots-farmhouses.html). A trade wiki gives the example that 15 farmhouse plots on a combined 3 acres need RERA registration "the same as any residential plotted development". — [FarmlandIndia](https://www.farmlandindia.com/wiki/farmhouse-lifestyle-land/farmhouse-projects-and-government-policies). (Both are secondary, non-official sources.)
- **Caution on the 99acres article:** It says registration is needed if a project "exceeds 500 sq m ... or involves more than eight plots". That is a looser reading than the statutory exemption wording above, so do not rely on it for the threshold. — [99acres](https://www.99acres.com/articles/rera-applicability-plots-farmhouses.html)
- **MP Rules territorial scope:** The MP Rules 2017 "shall apply to whole of the State of Madhya Pradesh". — [MP RERA Rules 2017, r.1(2) (ibclaw copy)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **MP Rules contemplate non-urban land.** Title documents may include a non-encumbrance certificate "from a revenue authority not below the rank of Tehsildar", and an NOC from the Nazul Officer (nazul land) "or ... from Sub-Divisional Officer (Revenue)". — [MP RERA Rules 2017, r.3(1)(d)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **MP Rules definition of "layout plan":** "a plan of the project depicting the division or proposed division of land into plots, roads, open spaces, amenities, etc." — [MP RERA Rules 2017, r.2(h)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **MP Rules exemptions from registration:** Renovation, repair or development that involves no marketing, advertising, selling or new allotment; structural repairs by a public authority; and rehabilitation buildings. — [MP RERA Rules 2017, r.4(1) second proviso](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **Rural colony framework running in parallel (not RERA):** The MP Gram Panchayat (Development of Colonies) Rules, 2014 apply in gram panchayat areas. A colonizer must register with the Competent Authority (registration valid for 5 years, renewable) and obtain development permission. — [legitquest, MP Gram Panchayat (Development of Colonies) Rules 2014](https://www.legitquest.com/act/madhya-pradesh-gram-panchayat-development-of-colonies-rules-2014/D3B2). Rural colony registration and permission are being moved onto the "Vyavastha" portal. — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/bhopal/mp-news-registration-of-colonies-in-rural-areas-through-vyavastha-portal)
- **Pending reform:** A draft "Integrated Colony Development Rules 2026" would extend the Urban Colony Development Rules 2021 to gram panchayat areas. A proposed "Colony Integration Act, 2026" would create a single licence for urban and rural colonies, with a uniform crackdown on illegal colonies within a 16 km radius of municipal limits. Neither was confirmed as in force. — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/bhopal/noose-on-illegal-colonies-to-tighten-as-uniform-law-to-crack-down-for-16-km-radius-from-municipal-limits); [Patrika, 9 Apr 2026, FIRs within 15 days for illegal village colonies](https://www.patrika.com/en/bhopal-news/strict-action-illegal-colonies-villages-fir-to-registered-within-15-days-20487676)
- **District-level action on agricultural-land plotting (Indore):** The district barred registration of small plots carved out of agricultural land to curb illegal colonies, and said colony plots may be booked only through RERA-registered agents. (The article is roughly 2.5 years old; current status unconfirmed.) — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/indore/indore-ban-put-on-sale-of-small-plots-developed-on-agricultural-lands-to-curb-illegal-colonies). Indore has also cancelled sale-deed registrations of plots in illegal colonies and required developers to refund buyers. — [Devdiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/science-environment/3435972-indore-cracks-down-illegal-colony-plot-registrations-annulled); [UrbanAcres](https://urbanacres.in/indore-cancels-unauthorised-plot-registrations-to-ensure-planned-city-growth/)
- **Other states have acted against farm-plot schemes:** Telangana RERA fined a promoter Rs 25.8 lakh for selling farm plots without registration, noting the land was still agricultural. MahaRERA has said non-farm use of plots is allowed only once infrastructure is in place. — [PropNewsTime (Telangana)](https://propnewstime.com/getdetailsStories/MjE0MjU=/telangana-rera-fines-sunrise-infra-properties-for-illegal-farm-plot-sales); [PropertyWala (MahaRERA)](https://propertywala.com/news/regulatory/non-farm-use-of-plot-only-after-infrastructure-in-place-maharera)

### Inferences
- A Jalkoti project that divides land of more than 500 sq m into more than 8 plots, with common roads and services, is a registrable "real estate project" whether it is marketed as "farmhouse plots" or as a "residential colony". Calling the plots "farmhouse" or "agricultural" does not take the project outside RERA if it is a common layout sold to many buyers.
- **"Planning area" risk:** Section 3(1) prohibits marketing without registration "in any planning area". If Jalkoti lies outside any notified planning area under the MP Nagar Tatha Gram Nivesh Adhiniyam 1973 (for example outside the Maheshwar development plan area), a promoter could argue registration is not mandatory. This is uncertain and risky. The Rules apply statewide, MP RERA in practice registers gram panchayat colonies (inferred from the rural revenue documents the Rules ask for), and the state is moving to unify rural and urban colony licensing. Prudent course: register.
- **Exemption reading:** On the literal "or" reading, a scheme of more than 500 sq m with 8 or fewer plots across all phases (for example a few large farmhouse plots) may be exempt. Splitting a larger scheme into phases does not help, because units are counted across all phases. Because the "both versus either" question is contested, confirm it in writing with MP RERA before relying on the exemption.
- A project with no RERA registration and no gram panchayat colony permission also exposes buyers to the district-level "illegal colony" measures: FIRs, refusal or cancellation of sale-deed registration, and demolition.

### Gaps
- I could not access rera.mp.gov.in, so I could not confirm whether MP RERA has issued any circular or order specifically on farmhouse, "farm land" or agricultural-plot schemes, or on gram panchayat areas. None was found in search results.
- I could not determine whether Jalkoti / Maheshwar falls within a notified planning area (T&CP development plan) for section 3(1).
- I could not confirm whether MP has used the section 3(2)(a) proviso to lower the 500 sq m / 8-unit threshold. Nothing in the 2017 Rules text does so.

## 2. Registration process on rera.mp.gov.in: documents, fee for plotted development, approval timelines, validity and extensions

### Takeaway
Under the MP Rules 2017, the promoter applies in Form A with Form B (the declaration), plus title and encumbrance proof, approvals, the layout plan, the plan of development works and three years of financials. The fee for plotted residential projects is Rs 10 × permissible FAR per sq m of plot area (green areas and roads excluded). The Authority must decide within 30 working days, though defect-rectification time and site inspection time are excluded. Registration runs for the completion period the promoter declares. Extension is in Form E, for a fee equal to the original registration fee, and is capped at one year.

### Cited Findings
- **Application:** Form A (in triplicate until the procedure became web-based) plus a Form B declaration. Form B must include an undertaking not to discriminate against any allottee on grounds of caste, creed, community or any other ground. — [MP Rules r.3(2), r.3(4)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **Documents under r.3(1), in addition to section 4 of the Act:**
  - (a) Name, photograph, contact details, PAN and Aadhaar of the promoter.
  - (b) Audited P&L, balance sheet, auditor's report and ITRs for the 3 preceding years.
  - (c) Number of open and covered parking areas.
  - (d) Title deed or other proof of title, or a non-encumbrance certificate from a revenue authority not below Tehsildar, plus an NOC from the Nazul Officer (nazul land) or the SDO (Revenue).
  - (e) Encumbrance details.
  - (f) Where the promoter is not the landowner: the owner's consent and the collaboration, development or JV agreement, with the owner's title documents.
  - (g) Other documents specified by regulations.

  — [MP Rules r.3(1)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **A later MP amendment concerns the copy of the registered collaboration, development or JV agreement** (amendment PDF title: "Amendment to MP Real Estate Regulation and Development Rules 2017"). I could not read its date or full content. — [uncomplycate.com PDF](https://uncomplycate.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/22.-Madhya-Pradesh_Amendment-to-MP-Real-Estate-Regulation-and-Development-Rules-2017.pdf)
- **Section 4(2) of the Act (Act text) also requires:**
  - Authenticated copies of approvals and the commencement certificate from the competent authority.
  - The sanctioned plan, layout plan and specifications.
  - The plan of development works and proposed facilities.
  - Location with boundaries and latitude/longitude.
  - The proforma allotment letter, agreement for sale and conveyance deed.
  - The number, type and area of plots.
  - Agents', contractors' and architects' details.
  - A declaration (affidavit) on title, encumbrances, the completion period and the 70% account.

  — [courtbook, s.4](https://courtbook.in/real-estate-regulation-and-development-act-2016/section-4); [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158)
- **For a Jalkoti colony, the "approvals" would typically include** the diversion order (land use change under the MP Land Revenue Code), the T&CP layout approval, and gram panchayat colony registration and development permission under the 2014 Rules (see Section 1). This mapping is my inference from r.3 and the rural colony framework. The specific MP RERA portal checklist was not accessible.
- **Disclosures on the project webpage (MP Rules r.14/15 list):**
  - Promoter profile and track record: projects completed in the past 5 years are compulsory, and all ongoing projects must be listed.
  - Audited balance sheet.
  - Advertisement or prospectus.
  - Approvals and commencement certificate.
  - Sanctioned layout plan.
  - Number, type and areas of plots.
  - Authorised agents with their registration numbers.
  - Consultants.
  - Location with boundaries and lat/long.
  - Development plan: water supply, electricity, external lighting, drainage and sewage treatment, fire-fighting, rainwater harvesting, renewable energy.
  - Gantt or milestone charts.
  - Site plan with khasra numbers and the area of each parcel.
  - Proforma allotment letter and agreement.

  — [MP Rules (website disclosure rule)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **Fee:**
  - "Ten rupees multiplied by the permissible Floor Area Ratio per square meter for all plots in plotted residential projects, including plots for common educational, health or recreational facilities, excepting green areas and roads."
  - Commercial or other non-residential plots: Rs 20 × FAR per sq m.
  - Mixed projects: pro rata.
  - Existing incomplete projects: three-fourths of the fee.

  — [MP Rules r.3(3)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/). Secondary confirmation that MP's plotted fee is Rs 10/sq m × FAR. — [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/insights/rera-madhya-pradesh); [Professional Utilities](https://www.professionalutilities.com/rera-registration/madhya-pradesh.php)
- **Withdrawal or rejection:** 10% of the fee is retained as a processing fee and the rest refunded within 30 days. — [MP Rules r.3(5)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **Decision time:**
  - The Authority must grant or reject within 30 working days. Where it allows defects to be rectified, the clock starts from the date of rectification. Registration is issued in Form C; rejection in Form D. — [MP Rules r.6(1), (2), (4)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
  - The Act (s.5) provides for deemed registration if there is no decision within 30 days (Act text, [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158)).
  - The MP Rules let the Authority inquire into the title, the layout plan, the promoter's financial, technical and managerial capacity, the plan of development works, and conformity with neighbouring areas. Time spent on such inquiries and site inspections "shall not be included in the time limit for deemed registration". — [MP Rules (Authority functions)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **Litigation on the Rule 6 proviso:** An Oct 2025 MP High Court judgment says reliance on the proviso to Rule 6 "to suspend the statutory period is contrary to" the Act. Only a snippet was available, so the full holding is unconfirmed. — [LatestLaws, MP HC Oct 2025](https://www.latestlaws.com/judgements/madhya-pradesh-high-court/2025/october/2025-latest-caselaw-10044-mp). The MP HC also set aside an MP RERA order over an improperly stamped registration application. — [K&S Partners](https://ksandk.com/newsletter/mp-high-court-nullifies-rera-order-on-unstamped-property-reg/); [Housing.com](https://housing.com/news/mp-high-court-sets-aside-mp-rera-order)
- **Practical delay:** A news headline reports that RERA registration "takes 15 days in Rajasthan, 4 months in MP". The article body was not read and the date is unconfirmed. — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/indore/indore-real-estate-regulatory-authority-registration-takes-15-days-in-rajasthan-4-months-in-mp)
- **Validity:** Registration is valid for the completion period the promoter declares under s.4(2)(l)(C) (Act s.5(3), [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158)).
- **Extension:**
  - Apply in Form E "not more than six months after and not less than three months prior to the expiry" (sic in source).
  - The fee equals the original registration fee. Applying less than 3 months before expiry adds a late fee of half the original fee.
  - Fees may be refunded at the Authority's discretion for force majeure or for extensions under 6 months.
  - The promoter must file an affidavit that the fees will not be charged to the project or recovered from allottees.
  - Extension may not exceed one year. Grant is in Form F; rejection in Form D after a hearing.

  — [MP Rules r.7](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)

### Inferences
- **Fee illustration:** If the applicable FAR for the residential plots is 1.25 and the saleable plot area is 20,000 sq m, the fee is 10 × 1.25 × 20,000 = Rs 2.5 lakh. Roads and green areas are excluded from the base. The FAR to use is whatever the T&CP or panchayat layout permits. I found no rural-specific FAR source.
- The section 3(1) bar on marketing applies until registration is granted. Allow for the realistic MP processing time, which reports put at months, not 30 days, before launching any sales campaign.

### Gaps
- The current online checklist, fee payment method and any revised fee schedule on rera.mp.gov.in could not be verified, because the site was blocked.
- I could not confirm whether the MP Rules 2017 have been amended (2018–2026) on fees, documents or timelines. Only one amendment PDF, on collaboration agreements, was located, and it was not read in full. The ibclaw text appears to be the original 2017 version.

## 3. Separate bank account (70% rule), withdrawal certification, quarterly updates and annual audit

### Takeaway
Seventy per cent of amounts collected from allottees must go into a separate scheduled-bank account and may be used only for land and development cost of that project. Withdrawals must be proportionate to completion and certified by an engineer, an architect and a practising CA. The accounts need an annual audit by a CA within six months of each financial year-end. MP additionally requires quarterly webpage updates within 10 working days of each quarter-end.

### Cited Findings
- **Section 4(2)(l)(D) (Act text):**
  - 70% of amounts realised from allottees must be deposited in a separate account with a scheduled bank within 15 days of realisation, and used only for construction and land cost of that project.
  - Withdrawals must be in proportion to the percentage of completion, certified by an engineer, an architect and a chartered accountant in practice.
  - Accounts must be audited within six months after each financial year by a practising CA, who verifies use of funds and the proportionality of withdrawals.

  — [iPleaders](https://blog.ipleaders.in/real-estate-regulation-and-development-act-2016-2/); [courtbook s.4](https://courtbook.in/real-estate-regulation-and-development-act-2016/section-4); [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158)
- **MP definitions:** "Land cost" is the cost incurred by the promoter, whether as an outright purchase, lease charges and so on. "Construction cost" is the cost incurred towards on-site expenditure for the physical development of the project. — [MP Rules r.5](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **Ongoing projects:** The 70% balance (collections less land, development and construction spend to date) must be deposited, and the bank account details disclosed. — [MP Rules r.4(5)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **Quarterly updates:** "The promoter shall upload the following updates on the webpage for the project within ten working days from the expiry of each quarter":
  - number and types of plots booked;
  - status of "construction of internal infrastructure and common areas with photographs";
  - status of approvals, whether received, applied for or to be applied for;
  - modifications to the sanctioned layout.

  — [MP Rules (quarterly update rule)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **The Authority may check penalties are not paid from the 70% account.** It may inquire whether penalty, interest or compensation was paid out of the 70% account or another project's funds, or recovered from allottees. — [MP Rules](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **Certification practice (secondary, ICAI material, largely Maharashtra-based):** An architect's certificate (Form 1), an engineer's certificate (Form 2) and a CA certificate (Form 3) accompany each withdrawal. The CA certifying withdrawals should differ from the statutory auditor. The annual compliance takes the form of a certificate rather than a full audit report. One deck notes that in MP and UP the promoter must give cost estimates and quarter-wise estimated progress at registration. — [WIRC-ICAI, RERA registration requirements](https://old.wirc-icai.org/images/material/RERA-registration-project-requirements.pdf); [WIRC-ICAI, Statutory audit under RERA](https://old.wirc-icai.org/images/material/Statutory-audit-under-RERA.pdf); [TaxGuru, RERA audit](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/rera-audit-2022.html)

### Inferences
- For a plotted colony, construction cost means internal development works: roads, drains, the water and power network, street lighting, parks. Withdrawals track the percentage completion of those works, so the engineer and architect certificates should be tied to the development-works plan and Gantt chart filed at registration.

### Gaps
- I could not verify the MP-specific withdrawal certificate formats, the annual audit report form, or the quarterly progress report format (or any MP RERA circular on them). In other states these are set by regulations or circulars.

## 4. Agent registration, advertising rules (marketing videos), plot-area disclosure, sale agreement, possession and defect liability

### Takeaway
No ad, video, social post or booking is allowed before the project is registered. After registration, every ad must carry the MP RERA registration number and the Authority's website. Brokers must be registered with MP RERA. The sale agreement must follow the MP model (Annexure A). The promoter cannot take more than 10% before a registered agreement for sale. Defects notified within 5 years of possession must be fixed free within 30 days.

### Cited Findings
- **Pre-registration marketing ban:** Section 3(1) bars any promoter from advertising, marketing, booking, selling, offering for sale or inviting purchase "in any manner" before registration (Act text, [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158)). Secondary sources confirm that in MP, projects cannot be advertised, sold or booked without registration. — [Godrej Capital](https://www.godrejcapital.com/media-blog/knowledge-centre/rera-madhya-pradesh); [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/insights/rera-madhya-pradesh)
- **MP rule on the registration number:** "The registration number shall necessarily be displayed by the promoter on all advertisements or marketing material, in whatever form, relating to the project, as well as in all the documentation with the allottee, including the Sale Agreement. Failure to do so shall attract penal action." — [MP Rules r.6(3)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **Advertisement contents and accuracy:** Section 11(2) requires ads and prospectuses to show the Authority's website address and the registration details. Under section 12, a promoter is liable to compensate an allottee who suffers loss from false information in an ad (Act text, [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158)).
- **The ad must be filed with the application.** The advertisement or prospectus issued for the project is a mandatory webpage disclosure. — [MP Rules](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **Agents:**
  - Every agent must register with MP RERA (Act s.9) on Form G with PAN, Aadhaar and other documents.
  - Fee: Rs 10,000 for an individual, Rs 50,000 for others.
  - Decision within 30 working days; certificate in Form H.
  - Renewal in Form J, 3 to 6 months before expiry; fee Rs 5,000 for an individual, Rs 25,000 for others.
  - Agents must "present a true and correct picture" and not mislead.

  — [MP Rules r.10–15](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/). The project webpage must list authorised agents with their registration numbers. — [MP Rules](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **Agents may not deal in unregistered projects.** Under section 10, an agent may not facilitate the sale of a plot in an unregistered project. Under section 62, an agent's default attracts Rs 10,000 per day, up to 5% of the cost of the plot (Act text, [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158)). Indore's district order required colony plot bookings to be made only through RERA-registered agents. — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/indore/indore-ban-put-on-sale-of-small-plots-developed-on-agricultural-lands-to-curb-illegal-colonies)
- **Plot-area disclosure:** "In case of plot development the promoter shall disclose the area of the plots being sold to the allottees" ([MP Rules r.4(4)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)). The webpage must also give "details of the number, type and areas of plots". — [MP Rules](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **Sale agreement:**
  - Must conform to the model form at Annexure A. Modifications are allowed only if they conform to the Act, and the Authority's decision on conformity is final. Non-conforming clauses are "ab initio void".
  - An application or allotment letter signed earlier does not limit the allottee's rights.

  — [MP Rules r.9](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/). Under section 13, no more than 10% of the cost may be accepted as an advance or application fee without a registered written agreement for sale (Act text, [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158)).
- **Interest and refunds:** The same interest rate applies to promoter and allottee, capped at SBI's highest MCLR + 2% (the Authority publishes the rate). Refunds with interest are due within 60 days. — [MP Rules r.17–18](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **Defects and possession:** Under section 14(3), a promoter must rectify any structural defect or defect in workmanship, quality or provision of services that the allottee reports within 5 years of possession, without charge, within 30 days. Under section 18, delay in possession entitles the allottee to a refund with interest, or interest for each month of delay. Under section 19(10), the allottee must take possession within 2 months of the occupancy or completion certificate (Act text, [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158)).
- **Complaints:** Form M (to the Authority) and Form N (to the adjudicating officer for compensation) each carry a Rs 1,000 fee. Appeals to the Tribunal cost Rs 5,000. — [MP Rules r.23–24, r.33](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)

### Inferences
- Marketing videos, drone walkthroughs, Instagram or YouTube reels, WhatsApp broadcasts and "pre-launch / EOI" collections for the Jalkoti project are "advertising" or "inviting persons to purchase in any manner". They must wait until the MP RERA registration certificate (Form C) is issued. After that, every video should show the registration number and the MP RERA website on screen.
- Any farmhouse-plot brochure promising amenities (clubhouse, plantation, roads) creates section 12 liability if those amenities are not delivered.

### Gaps
- I could not access the current MP RERA advertisement guidelines or circulars (for example on QR codes or font size of the registration number). Several states have issued such circulars; whether MP has could not be verified.

## 5. Completion: completion certificate from the competent authority, handover to association, the RERA project page, and MP-specific completion rules

### Takeaway
RERA ties "completion" to the certificate issued by the local competent authority. For a rural colony that would be the gram panchayat or other colony authority's development-completion certificate under the colony rules. The promoter uploads it to the project page, forms or enables the allottees' association, and executes conveyance deeds. I found no MP RERA-specific completion procedure for plotted colonies.

### Cited Findings
- **Definition:** Under section 2(q), a "completion certificate" is the certificate, by whatever name called, issued by the competent authority certifying that the project has been developed according to the sanctioned plan, layout plan and specifications (Act text, [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158)). The MoHUA FAQ distinguishes an occupancy certificate (habitable buildings with basic civic services) from a completion certificate (the whole project). — [MoHUA FAQs on RERA](https://mohua.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/FAQs-on-RERA(1).pdf)
- **Project page:** The promoter must upload an "authenticated copy of completion certificate and occupancy certificate, when these are due or application for them, if they have not been issued" ([MP Rules, website disclosures](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)). Quarterly updates continue until completion. — [MP Rules](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **Ongoing projects:** For MP's transition, projects were covered if they had not received a completion or occupancy certificate. — [MP Rules r.4(1)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/); [Corpbiz](https://corpbiz.io/learning/know-in-detail-about-rera-madhya-pradesh/)
- **Association of allottees (MP definition):** "a collective of at least two-thirds of the allottees ... registered under any law". — [MP Rules r.2(e)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **Act obligations on association and conveyance:** Section 11(4)(e) requires the promoter to enable formation of the association within 3 months of a majority of allottees booking. Section 11(4)(f) and section 17 require a registered conveyance deed in the allottee's favour, with common areas handed to the association, within the period set by local law, or otherwise within 3 months of the occupancy certificate. Section 11(4)(d) requires the promoter to provide and maintain essential services until the association takes over (Act text, [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158)).
- **MP deemed completion:** An MP RERA chief said in an interview that MP municipal law has a deemed-completion provision: if a completion application gets no response within 15 days, the project is treated as completed. That is a municipal (urban) provision. — [Housing.com interview](https://housing.com/news/interview-with-madhya-pradesh-rera-chief/)
- **Colony-rules pattern (state unconfirmed):** The developer reports completion of colony development works, the authority inspects and issues a work-completion certificate within 15 days, and the colony is then deemed transferred to the local body for maintenance. This is from an Indian Kanoon document whose state could not be confirmed. — [Indian Kanoon](https://future.indiankanoon.org/doc/28827448/)

### Inferences
- For Jalkoti, the relevant "completion certificate" for RERA purposes will almost certainly be the development-completion certificate under the MP Gram Panchayat (Development of Colonies) Rules 2014 (or the integrated colony rules, if notified), issued after the competent authority inspects the internal development works. MP RERA does not issue completion certificates itself. It records them on the project page and closes the project.
- Maintenance responsibility runs with the promoter until the association or gram panchayat takes over. The defect-liability clock (5 years) starts from possession of each plot.

### Gaps
- I could not access MP RERA's own project-closure or completion procedure (any form for the "end of project" declaration, or a final CA certificate) or any MP circular on plotted-colony completion.
- I could not confirm the exact completion and handover provisions of the 2014 Gram Panchayat colony rules, including any mortgage or bank-guarantee release of developed plots. These are likely covered in the colony-rules research stream.

## 6. Penalties for non-registration (section 59, up to 10% of project cost) and MP RERA enforcement examples

### Takeaway
Selling or marketing without registration attracts a penalty of up to 10% of the estimated project cost (s.59(1)). Continued violation can lead to up to 3 years' imprisonment, a further 10% fine, or both (s.59(2)). MP compounds that imprisonment for 10% of estimated project cost. MP RERA has imposed large non-registration penalties, including Rs 2.27 crore on an industrial project, upheld after a Supreme Court reversal of the High Court. I found no published MP RERA penalty specifically against a farmhouse-plot scheme.

### Cited Findings
- **Section 59 (Act text):**
  - s.59(1): contravening section 3 attracts a penalty up to 10% of the estimated cost of the project.
  - s.59(2): continued violation or non-compliance with orders attracts imprisonment up to 3 years, or a further fine up to 10%, or both.

  — [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158); secondary: [99acres](https://www.99acres.com/articles/rera-applicability-plots-farmhouses.html), and the UP RERA notice report via [Housing.com](https://housing.com/news/up-rera-issues-notice-to-m3m-over-rera-act-violation)
- **Other penalties (Act text):** s.60, false information or breach of s.4: up to 5%. s.61, other contraventions: up to 5%. s.62, agents: Rs 10,000 per day up to 5% of plot cost. — [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158)
- **MP compounding schedule:** Imprisonment under s.59(2) is compoundable for "10% of the estimated cost of the Real Estate Project"; the same applies to s.64. Imprisonment under ss.66 and 68 is compoundable for 10% of the cost of the plot or apartment. Compounding is conditional on complying with Authority or Tribunal orders within 30 days. — [MP Rules (compounding rule)](https://ibclaw.in/madhya-pradesh-real-estate-regulation-and-development-rules-2017/)
- **MP RERA Rs 2.27 crore penalty:** MP RERA penalised a developer for not registering an industrial project under ss.3 and 59. The Supreme Court set aside a High Court order that had quashed the penalty, and the Tribunal required a pre-deposit of 30% under s.43(5). Source is a search snippet; the article could not be fetched. — [LiveLawBiz](https://www.livelawbiz.com/rera/supreme-court-sets-aside-high-court-order-quashing-227-crore-rera-penalty-524082); also listed in the [LiveLawBiz RERA Half-Yearly Digest Jan–Jun 2026](https://www.livelawbiz.com/rera/livelawbiz-arbitration-half-yearly-digest-january-june-2026-539666)
- **MP High Court enforcement:** The MP HC directed the Bhopal Collector to enforce an MP RERA order for Rs 3.26 lakh within 10 days or appear in court. This shows RERA recoveries run through the Collector as arrears of land revenue. — [PropNewsTime](https://propnewstime.com/getdetailsStories/MTU3NDI=/hc-judgement-court-direct-bhopal-collector-to-enforce-inr-3-26-lakh-rera-order-in-10-days-or-appear-in-court)
- **Comparable non-MP actions on plots or farm plots:**
  - Bihar RERA: Rs 90 lakh penalty for marketing plots under a fake registration number ([LiveLawBiz](https://www.livelawbiz.com/rera/using-fake-rera-registration-number-to-lure-buyers-is-serious-fraud-bihar-rera-imposes-90-lakh-penalty-545302)).

### Inferences
